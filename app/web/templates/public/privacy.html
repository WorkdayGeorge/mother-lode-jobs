Privacy

You can browse every job without giving us anything. Here's what we keep when you choose to give it.

Job alerts

Your email address and the filters you chose. We use them only to send the alert. Unsubscribing stops the emails immediately.

Accounts

Your name, email, a scrambled copy of your password, the profile you fill in, and the jobs you save. If you upload a resume, we read the text out of it to match jobs and then throw the file away; only the text is kept.

If detailed reviews are turned on, a job description and your profile are sent to an AI service to write the review. They aren't used for anything else.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your account page. That removes your profile, resume text, matches and saved jobs right away.

We never sell your information or share it with employers. You decide when and where to apply.
